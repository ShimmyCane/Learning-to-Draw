# Visualize Image — Quality check (before a scene is published)

- [ ] The horizon is named in step 1 or 2, and drawn.
- [ ] The crop has a reason in one sentence.
- [ ] Step 2 has five to eight primitives, ordered back to front.
- [ ] Step 3 uses the masses as anchors; every secondary shape is measured against one.
- [ ] Step 4 states the vision and names what was left out.
- [ ] Every frame has a one-line rule a learner can repeat on their own image.
- [ ] Value frame uses at most five grays; color uses at most five colors.
- [ ] No address, no photo of people, no full names; the source photo is in `private/` and not committed.
- [ ] Any outside source is named as "inspired by" with one true line about it.
