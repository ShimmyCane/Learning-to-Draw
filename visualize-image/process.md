# Visualize Image — Process

Run these in order on any image. Each step ends with a frame and a one-line rule.

## 1. Crop and frame
1. Look at the whole image. List three things that are nice about it (a light, a shape, an overlap).
2. Find the contrast: the place where light meets dark, or big meets small. Put it near a third, not the middle.
3. Find the horizon (or the eye-level line in a room). Decide where it sits: low for sky or ceiling, high for floor.
4. Choose the crop. Write it as `x y w h` on the original, plus one sentence why.
5. Optional: suggest moves. "Shift the lamp left", "stand lower", "turn the chair". Keep them to three.

## 2. Mass
1. Draw the horizon line and the large background planes (wall, floor, window, sky).
2. Add the biggest objects as one primitive each: circle, oval, rectangle, triangle. Only five to eight in total.
3. Order layers back to front. Mark the three that will anchor the page (these are the references for step 3).
4. Check: squint at the frame. If the picture still reads, the masses are right.

## 3. Construct
1. Add secondary figures (lamp, plant, frame) in light lines, measured against the anchors ("a little less than half the sofa wide").
2. Add overlapping figures. Overlaps show depth: front shape hides the back one.
3. Rank everything 1 to 5. Priority 1 are the things the picture cannot live without.

## 4. Bold
1. Write the vision in one sentence: what is this drawing about?
2. Trace and thicken the outlines of priorities 1 and 2. Medium for 3. Thin or drop 4 and 5.
3. Name what was left back and why. This is a design choice.

## 5. Value and color
1. Squint and group everything into three to five values (light to dark). Block them flat.
2. Choose a small palette (three to five colors) and apply it by value. Keep the lightest light and darkest dark.
3. Output a frame for values and one for color.

## 6. Filters
Apply each lens to the final frame and keep the ones that teach something. See `schematic.md`.

## Making a scene
`data/scenes/<id>.json` as in `schematic.md`; the frames are drawn from it. Put the original photo in `private/` and note its name in `source`.
