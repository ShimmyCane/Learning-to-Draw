Six steps, seven sheets

  1. Crop and frame: find the best part, put what you love on a third.
  2. Big shapes first: the horizon, then only the biggest shapes.
  3. Add what sits on them: smaller shapes, then the ones that overlap.
  4. Make the lines bold: press hard on what matters, leave some out.
  5. Light to dark and a few colors.
  6. See it as dots, facets or lines.
