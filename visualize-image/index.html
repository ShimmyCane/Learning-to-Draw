Visualize Image

Take any image and learn to draw it, one step at a time.

  1. 1Crop and frame. What's interesting? Where's the contrast? Where's the horizon?
  2. 2Mass. The biggest shapes as flat layers, back to front. These are your anchors.
  3. 3Construct. Smaller figures, then overlapping ones, then rank what matters.
  4. 4Bold. Trace what serves the vision; let the rest fade.
  5. 5Value and color. Light to dark first, then a small palette.
  6. 6Filters. Dots, octagon dots, facets, only lines.
