# Visualize Image — lessons

## 2026-11-12 (round 1)
- **Ask for the big picture, not the objects.** A previous attempt to break a photo into primitives failed on horizons and backgrounds. Asking for a flat scene of simple shapes worked. Start from planes and masses.
- **A "3-D" request can come back as a flat layered scene.** That was a better teacher than perspective. Treat flat layers as the format.
- **The Index start-project skill is not always available.** In a session without the Index repo, build the folder from the Learning-to-Draw pattern.

## 2026-11-12 (round 1b)
- **Find the horizon from two receding edges.** Extend the counter edge and the bar top; they meet at eye level (about the upper shelf here). Better than guessing.
- **Scenes are `.js`, not `.json`,** so the pages work from `file://` with no server.
- **A layer marked priority 5 must not be a plane.** The wall was priority 5 and vanished from the value frame; planes are 4 at most.
- **Number badges collide.** Label only the shapes that matter in the priority frame.
