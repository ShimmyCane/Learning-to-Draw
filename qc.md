# Learning to Draw — Quality check (before a sheet is published)

- [ ] `node tools/check_sheets.js` passes.
- [ ] The title says what it is; the one-line instruction is clear to a grown-up reading it aloud.
- [ ] There is a finished example (black ink) before the child has to do it alone.
- [ ] Help fades: trace → dots → on your own, and the last row or box is theirs.
- [ ] Lots of white space; nothing crowds the edges, the header or the footer.
- [ ] Prints well in black and white: grey dashes visible but lighter than ink; no big black areas.
- [ ] Two-sided safe: no heavy fill (or marked "print one-sided" in the process).
- [ ] Nothing scary or unsuitable; no real person, logo or brand.
- [ ] "Inspired by" names the artist, teacher or source, and the `about` line tells something true about them in one line.
- [ ] Level fits: 1 = lots of help, 2 = some, 3 = none.
