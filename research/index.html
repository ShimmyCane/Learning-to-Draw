💭 What I was thinking (round 1)

Drawing starts with a moving line (Klee), then closed lines become shapes, and shapes join into things (Kandinsky's point → line → plane). So the bank is ten kinds that climb that ladder, five sheets each:

〰️ lines🔺 shapes🔢 dots🦋 mirror🔲 grid🔤 letters🧊 3-D🐱 things🧶 patterns🎨 finish it

Every guided sheet uses the same fade: look (black ink) → trace (grey dashes) → dots → on your own. The child always sees a finished example first, and the last row is theirs.

Levels are 1–3, not ages. Level 1 has the most help (a dotted starter in the "Your turn" box, dots on every mirror point). Level 3 has none.

Black and white on Letter portrait, with a lot of white space. Two-sided is fine for line sheets; the heavy pattern and letter fills are better one-sided.
